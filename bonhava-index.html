About the group

A strong foundation for growing businesses.

Bonhava Group Holdings is a parent organisation focused on developing, managing and supporting a diverse portfolio of businesses. We create connected opportunities while allowing each company to serve its market with purpose and independence.

From everyday wellbeing and style to property, hospitality and practical automotive services, our group is built around businesses that make a meaningful difference in the communities they serve.
