Create a professional corporate group website for Bonhava Group Holdings, a parent company that manages and presents a portfolio of subsidiary businesses. The website should establish Bonhava Group Holdings as a trusted, diversified organisation and clearly explain its group structure. Create a polished, modern, executive visual style with strong typography, refined spacing, professional imagery, and a confident but welcoming tone. Include a compelling homepage with the company value proposition, an overview of the group, key strengths, and clear calls to action. Add dedicated sections or pages for the subsidiary companies and business areas: Gym and fitness, Clothing and fashion shop, Coffee shop, Sports shop, Properties and real estate, Workshop, and Parts shop. Each subsidiary area should have a concise description, its role within the group, and space for relevant services, products, or contact details. Include About the Group, Our Companies, Group Values, Contact, and a professional footer with navigation and social/contact placeholders. Make the design easy to expand as more subsidiaries are added. Do not invent specific addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, financial claims, awards, client names, or business statistics; use clear placeholders where details are needed. Build the site as a corporate information and portfolio website rather than an online store at this stage.
